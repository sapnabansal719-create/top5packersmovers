Frequently Asked Questions (22 Answers)

Detailed answers on local & domestic shifting, packaging quality, transit insurance, hidden charges, and 7-step verification.

Local shifting charges typically range from ₹4,200 to ₹7,500 for a 1 BHK, ₹6,500 to ₹11,500 for a 2 BHK, and ₹9,500 to ₹16,500 for a 3 BHK depending on distance, floor level, lift availability, and packaging quality.

Domestic intercity moving rates depend on total volume of goods, road distance in kilometers, truck type (dedicated vs shared), toll taxes, and terrain difficulty such as hill routes in Himachal Pradesh.

Verified moving companies use multi-layer packaging including heavy-duty 3-ply and 5-ply corrugated sheets, air bubble wrap, foam sheets, waterproof stretch film, edge guards, and custom wooden crates for fragile marble or glass items.

Yes, transit insurance is strongly recommended for domestic moves. All-risk transit coverage usually costs 1.5% to 3% of the declared value and protects against accidental damage, fire, theft, or weather incidents during transit.

Inspect goods at delivery, note any damage on the delivery receipt or POD immediately, take clear photographs before unpacking completely, and submit the signed copy along with the policy number within 24 to 48 hours.

Cars are moved inside specialized enclosed hydraulic car carriers with wheel locks, while bikes are wrapped in foam, bubble sheet, and corrugated cardboard inside scratch-free two-wheeler cages or dedicated carriers.

For short local moves, bikes can sometimes travel in the same closed truck with proper partitioning, whereas for domestic moves cars are usually sent in dedicated car carriers for maximum safety and lower cost.

Always ask for an all-inclusive written estimate covering GST, toll taxes, floor/staircase carrying fees (if no lift), long-carry charges if the truck cannot park near the gate, and unpacking charges.

Booking 5 to 10 days in advance is ideal for local moves and 10 to 15 days ahead for domestic moves, especially during month-end, weekends, and peak summer relocation months.

Most verified movers allow free rescheduling if notified 48 to 72 hours prior to the pickup date. Cancellation terms vary by partner, so confirm the token refund policy in writing before paying an advance.

A physical or live video survey lets the moving company measure exact item volume, check fragile appliances, and verify parking/staircase access so the final quote stays accurate with no last-minute price surprises.

During rainy conditions, movers wrap cartons in waterproof LDPE stretch film, use moisture-resistant closed-body containerized trucks, and elevate boxes on wooden pallets inside the vehicle.

Most full-service movers provide trained technicians for basic dismantling and reassembly of beds, wardrobes, LED TVs, and washing machines, while split AC uninstallation and gas-locking can be added at nominal technician charges.

Office moves use anti-static bubble wraps for monitors and servers, color-coded crate labeling by department, and weekend or overnight scheduling so your business downtime stays near zero.

Yes, if your new home is not ready yet, verified logistics partners provide 24/7 CCTV-monitored, pest-controlled, and palletized storage warehouses on flexible weekly or monthly billing.

Typically, a small token of 10% to 20% confirms your slot, 60% to 70% is paid upon loading completion with the lorry receipt (LR), and the remaining balance is settled upon safe delivery.

A dedicated truck carries only your belongings directly from origin to destination for fastest delivery, while shared/part-load transport splits space with other shipments to reduce costs by 25% to 40% for smaller loads.

A 1 BHK typically takes 3 to 4 hours, a 2 BHK takes 5 to 7 hours, and a 3 BHK usually takes 7 to 9 hours with a crew of 4 to 6 trained packers.

Hazardous and flammable goods such as LPG cylinders, petrol, paint thinners, crackers, perishable food items, cash, gold jewelry, and original legal documents should not be packed in the moving truck.

Check their active GSTIN on the official portal, verify physical office address, confirm IBA approval code (where applicable for bank/PSU reimbursement), and insist on a stamped bill and consignment note (LR).

Yes, demand spikes around the 25th to 5th of every month and on Saturdays/Sundays. Moving mid-week (Tuesday to Thursday) or mid-month can save 10% to 20% on overall moving costs.
